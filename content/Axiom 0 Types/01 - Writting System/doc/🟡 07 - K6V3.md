for Roundness, it is a simple vertical mark.

for every vowel here, 
it's Openess part is on the left and
it's Frontness part is on the right and horizontally flipped
This means that both the Openess part and the Frontness part can share the same stem
By convention, the Roundness marking is on the right side of the glyph, but marking it on the left is not wrong as well
