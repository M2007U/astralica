In grammar for most languages we know​
- the Subject is the one who applies an action
- the Verb is the action performed by the Subject
- the Medium is the secondary object such that it helps or modify the verb
- the Object is the one who accept the action

## Active Voice

a sentence in Astralica should be structured as :

```
<Særia-Marker><...> <Særia-Marker><...> <Særia-Marker><...> <Særia-Marker><...>
```

if there are no Særia-Markers *(i.e. all Særia-Markers are omited)*, then the sentence is

```
<SUB> <VERB> <MED> <OBJ>
```

## Passive Voice and other choices

both [[🪴 02b _ Forutaxi _ Flesh]] and [[🪴 02b _ Tækizamæ _ Cyborg & Electronics]] handles this differently

### Forutaxi

since each chunk is accompanied with a Særia-Marker
we simply move the `<OBJ>` chunk to the first
if we want to emphasize a chunk, we move the chunk to the front, but with a Særia-Marker along with it.

### Tækizame

in this case, we maintain the circular structure of the sentence :

```
SUB --> VERB --> MED --> OBJ
```
 
 and we will perform some numbers of right rotate until we have the chunk that we want to emphasize is the 1st chunk

>[!example] I cut an apple with a knife
>```
>SUB -> VERB -> MED -> OBJ : I am the one who cut the apple, with a knife
>VERB -> MED -> OBJ -> SUB : i CUT the apple, with a knife
>MED -> OBJ -> SUB -> VERB : i cut the apple, not with anything, WITH A KNIFE
>OBJ -> SUB -> VERB -> MED : i cut THE APPLE, NOT ANYTHING ELSE, with a knife
>```
