📢 M-ME ?!!! N-N-NO WAY !!!
✏️ Koixino ?!!! QuNo !
🗒️ /QuNo/ here literally means "False", but in this context, it can simply mean "NO"



📢 THAT'S IMPOSSIBLE 
✏️ 
```
Sæ Svik Xino
Tæyu
Ji-Poba
Kiamasæni
No
```
🗒️ 
- Here it can be literally translated to "The probability of this event is zero", or word by word "Event InfoSingle This Possessive-Attach Probability Equal Zero"
- /Ji-Poba/ is a loan word for "Probability"
- /No/ is the cropped version of /Kla-No/



📢 HMPH ~ (extremely blushes)
✏️ HMPF ~ ( Koixino Kræt LiTarakaRahanota Skla Krat )
🗒️ here /LiTarakaRahanota/ means the color "red", /Skla/ means "crank-up", here we will turn this "redness" into a verb by wrapping with /Kræt ... Krat/



📢 W-W-WHAT NOW ?!!!
✏️ Tæræf Takako Tæraf SvakKrut
✂️ Takako SvakKrut
🗒️ 
- /Takako/ means "now", which is a value of a moment, which normally won't be used as a noun, so grammatically it should be wrapped with /Tæræf ... Tæraf/
- /SvakKrut/ can be treated as "Request Action", or "What it is doing ?"

- /Hia-Skla-æra/ : temperature is cranked high and outside of comfort zone
