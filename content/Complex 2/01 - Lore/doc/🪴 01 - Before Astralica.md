
# Once upon a time

Before Astralica existed,
there were four civilizations:
- **Qion**
- **Elgiar**
- **Hino**
- **Logos**

Although they were different from one another,
the four civilizations had established contact and cooperation and alliance.
In particular, **Elgiar and Hino** had been working together on an ambitious project

**SkyNode**
*a massive moving city designed to travel through the sky without a concrete formation.*

Qion and Logos also contributed to the project,
providing technology, experiment results, knowledge, and resources.
SkyNode was originally intended to be a city of the future rather than a means of escape.
now 7 days left until construction complete

Then **Damɑhqɣiyɑ began its blitz-krieg attack.**












---
# the Fall of Qion

Qion was attacked first.

The attack came suddenly,
and Qion was unable to send a warning to the other civilizations
before its communications were destroyed.
With their land becoming unsafe,
the surviving people of Qion sought shelter with **Logos**,
hoping that the attack would not reach them.

But Damɑhqɣiyɑ continued its advance.

---

# the Fall of Logos

Logos soon came under attack as well.

With Qion already taking shelter there, Logos realized that the attacks were not isolated incidents. Damɑhqɣiyɑ was systematically conquering one civilization after another.

Before their communications were cut off,
Logos managed to send warnings and requests for help to **Hino and Elgiar**.

The message was simple:
**Damɑhqɣiyɑ was coming. Qion and Logos need help.**


---

# Noah's arch : Sky Node

At this point, 3 days left until Sky Node is complete.
But now since Elgiar and Hino knows Damɑhqɣiyɑ is now launching Blitz-krieg

What was originally intended to become a moving city was suddenly transformed into something much more desperate:
**an escape vessel.**
