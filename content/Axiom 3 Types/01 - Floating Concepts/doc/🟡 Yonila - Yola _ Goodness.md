good, kindness, excellence, anything that is good falls under this radical / category
basically, /Yonila/ can be simply translated to "good"

However, /Yonila/ is abstract and subjective, so it cannot be treated as a derivation of an existing Axiom Type.

>[!note] Pronunciation Inspiration
>- from the Chinese word "you" (优)
>- from the Chinese word "liang" (良)
>- from the English word "Nice"

>[!note] Glyph derivation
>inspirated by the Chinese charracter "liang" (良)

# the Opposite : Yonilu > Yolu _ Bad

here we flip /a/ to /u/ to show the total opposite of Good
"bad", "worse", "evil", "disaster", "catastrophic", etc, falls under this category
