The symbol is derived from the shape of a box or a package.​
Mainly take cares some of the properties that an object can have, the color, size, etc.

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>the name of this fundamental type is derived from /Kosra/ and /no/.
> /Kosra/ is one of the fundamental Types
> /no/ is the number 0.
> 
> In the past, this fundamental type is called /Kosrano/,
> but after time, the name got compressed and slightly rearranged to /Krosno/,
> but at some point it got tidied and now it is cropped to /Ko/
> 
> /KosraNo/ mainly has the meaning of "zero Kosra", or "no Kosra" or "an entity that has no [[🟡 01 _ Kosra - Koa _ NULL]]", which is basically a normal non-living-object.
