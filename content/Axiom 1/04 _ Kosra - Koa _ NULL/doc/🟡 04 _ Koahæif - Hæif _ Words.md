information can be transfered or spread or recorded in either written text, or said words.
/Koahæif/ refers to both written text or spoken words.

## Written or Spoken

to specify if we are refering to "written words" or "spoken words"
we can attach /Fi/ or /Hæ/ at the back

## Read, Listen, Write, Speak

both Read and Listen are considered receiving information
and Write and Speak are considered providing information
