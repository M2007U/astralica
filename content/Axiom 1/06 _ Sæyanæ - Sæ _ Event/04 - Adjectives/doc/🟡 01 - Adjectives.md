
## Cranking the Slider

Most of the Adjectives can be categorized into 2 groups :
one express some excessiveness of some trait
Then, the other one expresses some lack of certain traits.

*to some extend, these 2 are actually quite equivalent it is just that each of them are on both sides on a spectrum*

Here we have ways to crank a trait
- One Excessive
- one Suitable and Neutral
- one Lacks

## Objective Relative Describing *(Comparing)*

Just like in English, Adjectives can become comparative by adding "er" and become superlative by adding "est"

Astralica uses the concept of a [[🟡 02 _ Kiara Maji - Kiama _ Scale or Balance]] to decide which 2 entity has more of that trait than the other

*this will also work with Crank-Low and Crank-Medium as well*

>[!example]
>consider the following statement :
>**/ A Kaklana-Sklæmara B /**
>
>/Kaklana/ : refers to "the Quantification of Space in 3D", in other words, "size"
>/Sklæ/ : refers to the lack of size, in other words, "smallness"
>/mara/ : means the noun on the right *(which in this example is B)* has more "smallness"
>
>translated to English it can be "B is smaller than A"

## Adjective to Verb

When an adjective is used as a verb,
then the "skl(a/o/æ)( (r(a/æ/i/o/u))/(mæ/ma/ta/t)æ)" segment should go to the front instead.
if this adjective is used inside a [[🟡 02 - Kosra Information Segment]], then the segment stays at the back to act as a "Kosra information Segment Item value"

If the adjective uses Subjective Describing,
then it is not transitive because it is only describing itself

If the adjective uses Objective Relative Describing,
then it is transitive because it is being compared to another noun,
an Object is expected
