# (1) - Chaotic

usually people uses a slightly shorter form in the daily lives. In this case, there is no pattern, and also, it is context and culture heavy. So by using this approach, we will have a lot of words that do not follow a certain.

This can mean that all the verbs that appears in the [[🟡 01 _ Jaxitæ - Ja _ Chaotic Dictionary]] falls under this category.

most of the time, these verbs will not contain the prefix /Kruoiztæ/
but if you decided to concretely state that a word is a Body Part verb, you can still attach /Kruoiztæ/

---

# (2) - Systematic

one way is to systematically describe the verb
in this case, these words will hold the structure below :

Slot 1 : get or give : /sva/ or /svæ/ , can refer to [[🟡 03 - Giving and Receiving]]
Slot 2 : target : what we are dealing with ?
Slot 3 : ok or yuck : is it ok to talk about or is it gross ? /Yo/ or /Bu/ , can refer to [[🟡 02 _ Yonila - Yola _ Goodness]]
