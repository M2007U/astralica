
Sometimes, an entity or an event can cause some event to happen, or the other way (a.k.a. avoiding it to happen, to prevent).

Causing and Preventing are verbs to encourage or discourage events to happen.
They are verbs [[🟡 01 _ Karuta - Krut - Ku _ Verb]] , so they have the /Krut/ prefix, they act on events [[🟡 01 _ Sæyanæ - Sæ _ Event]] , so they have the "Sæ" segment

the on the encourages is "1"(/ni/),
the one the discourages is "0"(/no/)
