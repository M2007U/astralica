Sometimes we need to temporarily points / refer to
a location, object, living being, and perhaps other things
in fancier words : Demonstrative

The symbol is derived from the shape of a pointer / cursor

>[!note] Evolution Tracking
>/Xiræf/ is a derivation of [[🟡 01 _ Tæga - Tæ _ Noun]]

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>"Shisha" from Japanese and "Refer" from English

>Objects : This, That, These & Those
