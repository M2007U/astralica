## "is, equal"

like in English we have the words "is", "am", "are", "was", "were", "will be"
in Astralica, we have /kiama - sæyanæ - ni/ > /Kiamasæni/
the "Sæyanæ" part can be interpreted as "the Scale is BEHAVING in some way",
but then "what way ? which way ?"
in this case, it is the "ni" way
