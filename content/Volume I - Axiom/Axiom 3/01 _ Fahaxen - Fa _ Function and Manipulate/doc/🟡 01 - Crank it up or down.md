Slot1 is the main word we want to manipulate
Slot2 is either /skla/ or /sklæ/
Slot3 is a [[🟡 04 - Prime Factor Script]] number to describe "how far we crank it"

>[!example] the rain is getting WORSE QAQ
> refer to [[🔵 aramæin]]

there are 2 ways to crop this long sentence:
- for this special case, we will crunch
	/Fahaxen/ + /Skla/ into /Flask/ and
	/Fahaxen/ + /Sklæ/ into /Flæsk/
	and then put if after the word we want to manipulate, then followed by a [[🟡 04 - Prime Factor Script]]
- we can use /Flask/ or /Flæsk/ as a post fix for the word, and every post-fix-attaching will crank up/down the value
