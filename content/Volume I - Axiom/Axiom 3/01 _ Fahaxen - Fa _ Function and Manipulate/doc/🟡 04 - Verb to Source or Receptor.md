sometimes we have a transitive verb, and sometimes we want the subject or the object.

>[!example]
>say we have the verb "eat",
> if we try to grab the subject, we will get "eater"
> if we try to grab the object, we will get "food"

Slot 2 : /Sæara/ for "Subject" or /Sæaru/ for "Object", refer to [[🟡 01 - Basic Sentence Structure]]

to crop the pronunciations
for /Faræ ... Faro Sæara Fara/, it can be /... Fasæara/
for /Faræ ... Faro Sæaru Fara/, it can be /... Fasæaru/

>[!example]
>take the verb /[[🔵 nomu]]/
>/ nomu-Fasæara / becomes "the one who eats"
>/ nomu-Fasæaru / becomes "food"
