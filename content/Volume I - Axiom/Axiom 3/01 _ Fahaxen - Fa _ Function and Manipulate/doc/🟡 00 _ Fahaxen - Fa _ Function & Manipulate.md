this is a mechanism to change the meaning of a word
it works with nouns, verbs, and adjectives.

/Fahaxen/ also has the meaning "to change, or to process something"

>[!note] Glyph Derivation
>derived from the shape of a blender
>and it is mentioned here : https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3geaNq-82DU

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>from the English word "Function"
>from the Chinese word "Hua"

the way we use it is to use the following structure :

![[fahaxen.png\|32]]![[KaxiRae.png\|32]]![[empty_128.png\|64]]![[AstralicaSlots_Number-1.png\|64]]![[empty_128.png\|64]] ![[fahaxen.png\|32]]![[KaxiKo.png\|32]]![[empty_128.png\|64]]![[AstralicaSlots_Number-2.png\|64]]![[AstralicaSlots_Number-3.png\|64]]![[empty_128.png\|64]]![[fahaxen.png\|32]]![[KaxiRa.png\|32]]

/Faræ (Slot1) Faro (Slot2)(Slot3) Fara/

where Slot1 is the word we want to manipulate or alter or modify
and Slot2 is the operation we want to use which is the part we define how to modify the word
and Slot3 is the value to describe how is the manipulation is done

sometimes in casual speak,
we can omit /Faræ/ and /Fara/
and we can treat /Faro/ as : will manipulate the word before it based on the given information behind it
