The symbol is derived from us, "humans".​

Krosni is the opposite of Krosno, "entity with Kosra"
Mainly take cares some of the properties that an living being can have, the pronouns, body parts, etc.

>[!warning] /KosraNi/ is not only just Humans
>any living being falls under /KosraNi/, technically speaking, /KosraNi/ can be treated as "Biology"
>

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>the name of this fundamental type is derived from /Kosra/ and /ni/.
> /Kosra/ is one of the fundamental Types
> 
> where "ni" is the number 1. ([[🟡 01 _ Klari - Kla _ Quantification]])
> 
> In the past, this fundamental type is called /Kosrani/,
> but after time, the name got compressed and slightly rearranged to /Krosni/,
> but at some point got tidied and now is cropped to /Koi/
> 
> /Kosrani/ mainly has the meaning of "has /Kosra/" or "an entity that has [[🟡 01 _ Kosra - Koa _ NULL]]",
> Which is basically a living being.
