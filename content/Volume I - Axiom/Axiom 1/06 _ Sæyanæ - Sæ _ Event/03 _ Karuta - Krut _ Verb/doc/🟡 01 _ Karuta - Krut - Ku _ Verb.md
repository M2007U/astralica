So in Astralica, /Krut/ has the meaning of "move", "verb", "movement", "motion" etc

the original symbol (left) for "krut" is derived from 3 symbols
[[🟡 01 _ Kaxi - Ka _ Space]] , "Ru" (division), [[🟡 01 _ Taxi - Ta _ Time]] 

after a few iterations, the symbol (right) is constructed based on the "onion skin" effect

for both cases, adding the [[🟡 01 _ Sæyanæ - Sæ _ Event]] symbol on the left is OPTIONAL

this means that if a root of a word is a verb, then that word has a radical of "Krut"

## Turning a noun or a noun clause into a verb

Sometimes, verbs can be derived from things or descriptions.
Based on how a noun is being used, the verb can be derived from the object.
Based on what does an adjective trying to explain, the verb can be derived from the adjective.
Sometimes, an entire event can also be turned into a verb as well

To do so, we just need to wrap the noun or adjective with
/Kræt/ ... /Krat/

>[!example]
>the word "Knife" is a noun, when turned into a verb, it becomes the word "cut"
> the word "Eye" is a noun, when turned into a verb, it becomes the word "see" or "watch" or "look", where those subtle differences can be added with extra information segments
