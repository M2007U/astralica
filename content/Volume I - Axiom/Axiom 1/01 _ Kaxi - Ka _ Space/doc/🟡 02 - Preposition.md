## Slot 2 : Dimension Quantity

Standing in a line, Pointing on a paper, chilling in a room, these 3 situations are all in 3 different Environment Dimension.

Standing in a line, a line only has forward or backward when queueing, or left and right when taking a selfie with your friends is a 1D situation.

Pointing on a map while playing D&D is a 2D situation, flying a drone or shopping in a gigantic mall is a 3D situation.

By attaching the number of Dimensions we are dealing with by using Prime Factor Script, we can know how many dimensions is this word dealing with. To pronounce it, pronounce the number as
"ni" (1D) or "næ" (2D) or "na" (3D)
