
## Asking

same as Chinese, we replace the subject or object or whatever we want to ask with a question word and then expecting the received answer to be a valid one to replace the question word in the sentence

>[!example]
>EN : What did you eat ?
>ZH : 你吃了什么？
>Lit : You eat what ?
>
>here we can see
> "You" is the subject
> "eat" is the verb
> "what" is the blank we want the others to fill it in.

## Answering

during everyday conversation, when a speaker answers the question, he/she only provides what should fill in the blank.

>[!example]
>"What did you eat ?"
>"KWASOHNT 🥐🥐🥐"

but if you are let's say taking an exam or you are in a formal situation, 
answer words are to some extent required, but it is as simple as :
change /Svak/ to /Svæk/
