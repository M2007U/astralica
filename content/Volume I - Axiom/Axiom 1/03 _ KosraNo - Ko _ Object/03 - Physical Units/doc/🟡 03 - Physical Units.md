# Combining Units

When Units are combined, you can end up with a different unit,
In Astralica, we call this "Composite Units"

>[!example]
>Position is a "Pure Unit" (made from Kaxi only)
>
> Velocity is a "Composite Unit" (made from Kaxi and Taxi) where Velocity is "Length Divided by Time"
> 
> Acceleration is also a "Composite Unit" ("Change in Velocity over time")

Since Units are combined based on Multiplication and Division,
We can clump several fundamental Units together by using the "Ru" particle.
In this case, we can treat the "Ru" particle as a Fraction line.
Everything before "Ru" is the Nominator of that Composite Unit.
Everything after "Ru" is the Denominator of that Composite Unit.

## Energy - /Kosrano - Woki/ > /KoWo/ > /Wo/ - the one that cannot be Destroyed, nor Created

Since Energy can be converted from one form to another form, Energy can be treated as a gate way to convert one set of Pure Units into another set of Pure Units

And this is what makes "Energy" special to the other Pure Units
So "Energy" has it's own symbol and name :

![[AEwa - Energy.png|50]]![[empty_128.png|50]]![[c-81-w.png|25]]![[v-321-o.png|50]]![[c-63-k.png|25]]![[v-160-i.png|50]]


>[!note] Glyph Evolution
>the symbol for "Woki" is derived from a box ( a rectangle ) being pushed ( the arrow ) for a certain distance ( the tick mark ), according to physics, the scenario above describes "Work", which is equivalent to energy
>
>![[AEwa - Energy - Evolution.png|250]]

Sometimes we would like to specify which type of Energy it is, in this case we can use "Woki", then attach the set of pure units behind it.

>[!warning]
>
>when "Woki" is used,
> it means that we are describing which Energy it is, referring to a type of energy
> 
> when "Wo" is used,
> Energy is a part of the Composite Unit

>[!example]
>Recall that $Q = mc ΔT$
> Specific Heat Capacity (c) can be treated as
> "Energy divided by mass and temperature change", or "Ko - WoRuMaHi"
> 
> if we want to specify or describe the energy we are trying to say is "Heat energy",
> then we might say "Ko - Woki - Hiara" *(Energy, what type of Energy ? Temperature Energy)*
> 
> Here are some more Energy Examples :
> Kinetic Energy : /Ko - Woki - KaRuTa/ (Energy based on Velocity)
> Gravitational Potential Energy : /Ko - Woki - Kaxi/ (Energy based on Position)

>[!note] Energy wasn't /Woki/ or /Kowo/
>In the past, "æwa" means "energy", and the pronunciation can be cropped to "wa". However, somewhere afterwards, we came up with a new Emergence Type : "Warak", which can also be cropped to "wa". In this case, it causes confusion and sometimes ambiguity.
