## Vowels

after some feed backs, it is more user friendlier if instead of formants,
we use "Open, Front, Rounded" as the 3 parameters, just like IPA.



for "open-ness" and "front-ness", we will use the following group of glyphs
the Openess part is on the left and
the Frontness part is on the right and horizontally flipped
This means that both the Openess part and the Frontness part can share the same stem
By convention, the Roundness marking is on the right side of the glyph, but marking it on the left is not wrong as well
