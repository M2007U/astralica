>
>| EN    | ZH    | JP      |
>| ----- | ----- | ------- |
>| left  | zuo   | hidari  |
>| right | you   | migi    |
>| up    | shang | uæ      |
>| down  | xia   | shita   |
>| front | qian  | massugu |

>here Chinese Charracters at least convey some sense of direction the their glyphs but the pronunciation is a mess
