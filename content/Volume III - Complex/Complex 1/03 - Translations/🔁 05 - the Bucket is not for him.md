*original sentence*
"if he sees the bucket, he will believe it is for him"

so in this case, we might have to turn it into
"he owns it"

```

	he
	sees
	bucket Tædaf-Tagging BETA

	he
	belive

		BETA
	NounClauseEnd
if-End
```
