

# New Begining

The survivors of Qion, Elgiar, Hino, and Logos remained together aboard SkyNode.

They had lost their homes, their cities, and many of their people.
They could no longer simply return to the civilizations they once knew.

So they decided to begin again.
SkyNode was no longer merely a city.
It was now their **home**.

From time to time, beings from other worlds would visit them.
- Some would trade with them
- some would share knowledge
- some would eventually become part of their growing society.

As their civilization developed, however, the survivors encountered an unexpected problem :

They had inherited many different systems of measurement, terminology, conventions, and languages from the old civilizations.

- Some units had been defined for historical reasons.
- Some measurements were based on arbitrary standards.
- Some concepts existed simply because their ancestors had decided that they should exist.
- some systems did not make sense anymore.

The people and community began to question them.
- Why should a unit be defined this way?
- Why should this word spelt this way?
- Why should different civilizations use different systems for describing the same thing?

They wanted something more consistent.
Something that could be understood by everyone living aboard their new home.

---

# the Standardization

The survivors began creating a standardized system of communication.

They attempted to remove unnecessary irregularities inherited from their old civilizations and establish clear, consistent rules.

The goal was not to erase the languages of Qion, Elgiar, Hino, or Logos.
Instead, the goal was to create a common language that everyone could learn.

A language designed for their **new civilization**.
This language eventually became known as **Astralica**.

However,
the people did not want their new standard to completely eliminate regional speech.
Different communities continued to develop their own ways of speaking.

These became recognized dialects, including:
- regional
	- [[🪴 01a _ Qion - Qionpa]]
	- [[🪴 01a _ Elgia - Elgiarian]]
	- [[🪴 01a _ Logos - Logese]]
	- [[🪴 01b _ Elgia & Hinorean - RutTæk]]
	- [[🪴 01a _ Hinorian - Nyarato]]
- species
	- [[🪴 02a _ Hiureni _ Human]]
	- [[🪴 02a _ Kæmaganæ _ Felis]]
	- [[🪴 02a _ Kainuqu _ Canids]]
	- [[🪴 02a _ Ariatovi _ Avians]]

Astralica became the common standard, while the dialects preserved parts of the cultures and identities that survived the old world.

# renaming Sky Node

Eventually, SkyNode itself was renamed.
The new name was constructed from concepts associated with the structure of SkyNode:

[[🟡 01 _ Kaxi - Ka _ Space]] + [[🟡 01 _ Kikuo - Kio _ Tree]] + [[🟡 01 _ Warak - Wa _ Testalation]]

which was shortened into:
**Ka + Kio + Wa**

and eventually became:
**Kawakio**

The survivors also created a name representing the four civilizations that had come together aboard the vessel.

The names of the four civilizations were shortened:
**Qion + Elgiar + Hino + Logos**
became:
**Qi + El + Hi + Lo**
forming:
**Elhiloqi**

Together, these names represented the new home and the people who created it.
**Elhiloqi Kawakio.**
