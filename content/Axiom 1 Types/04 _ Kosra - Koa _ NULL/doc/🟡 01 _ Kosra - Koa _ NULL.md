>[!warning] Kosra is a unique concept which other languages does not have
> which means it cannot be directly translated into any other words in English, Japanese or Chinese, instead if can be a combination of a few concepts that we are used to.

Kosra has the meaning of
"Mind", "Concept", "Information", "Data", "Intelligence", "Knowledge", "Passion", "Idea".

The symbol is derived from "putting/retrieving an idea into a flask"

>[!hint]
>/Kosra/ sometimes can slightly mean or hint "information" or "data", not in a literal way but in a semantic / grammatical way. But remember, /Kosra/ is /Kosra/, "information" is "information"
