## Slot 2 : Space Segment List

An array of vertical strips (Space Segments), each strip will carry a quantity in terms of space, the total quantity is the sum of the values from each Space Segments

Here is what a Space Segment should look like :

|                 |                                                                                                                         |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Numerator**   | A Number written in [[🟡 04 - Prime Factor Script]] to describe How many pieces of divided unit of space is represented |
| **Unit Symbol** | which unit of space we are using ?                                                                                      |
| Denomenator     | A number written in [[🟡 04 - Prime Factor Script]] to describe how many pieces we are dividing the unit into ?         |

## Length Units


![[KosraNo_Lira.png\|50]]
**Distance of Light : /Ka - Kla - Li/**
The distance for Light to travel in 1 Kona second.

which is approximately :
$$
\begin{align*}
&3. ~ 0237 ~ 6084 ~ 4852 ~ 9519 s \times 299 ~ 792 ~ 458 ~ m/s \\
&= 9. ~ 0650 ~ 0696 ~ 0826 ~ 2309 ~ 8656 ~ 7702 \times 10^8 ~ m \\
&= 9 ~ 0650 ~ 0696 ~.~ 0826 ~ 2309 ~ 8656 ~ 7702 ~ m
\end{align*}
$$

![[03 - KoNa - Atom.png|50]]
**Distance of Atom : /Ka - Kla - KoNa/**
The distance of 2^32 ground state Hydrogen atom diameters,

which is approximately :

$$
0.1058 ~ nm ~ \times ~ 2^{32} ~=~ 0.~4544~0753~9916~8~m
$$
