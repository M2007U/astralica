The symbol is derived from the 3 perpendicular axes in a euclidian environment.
If you are familiar with Unity or Blender or 3D modeling software, you usually see that as a gizmo.

This symbol can represent "position" or "direction".

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>from "**k**oŋ" from Chinese
> "sp**ace**" from English,
> after concatenation of the bold part, we have "Kace"
> taking this result and "Japanize beaming" it, we can have "Kaisu",
> "Japanize beaming" it again we have /Kaxi/

## Influence in Grammar and Lexicon

Since Astralica aims for precision and "anti-ambiguity", one approach is to have some grammatic words or particles to come in pair, like a parenthesis. These particles are often modified with the mind where :

"æ" is for the left side, "a" is for the right side,
just like how we have "KaxiRæ" and "KaxiRa" here.

>[!example]
>In a later chapter "Tæga - Noun", to turn a phrase or event into a noun, we will wrap the phrase or event with
> /TæRæf/ ... /TæRaf/
> 
> both /TæRæf/ and /TæRaf/ are derived from /Tæga/ which refers to "a tag", or "a name", or "a noun"
