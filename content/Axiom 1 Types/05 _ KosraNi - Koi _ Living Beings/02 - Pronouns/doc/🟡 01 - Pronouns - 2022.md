## Slot : Biological Gender

for an individual,
it can be "not male" or "male".
it can also be "not female" or "female".
This means that we can have 4 combinations.


| /n-/     | /t-/ | /-o/       | /-a/   |
| -------- | ---- | ---------- | ------ |
| not male | male | not female | female |

To specify gender in an Astralica Pronoun,
we can use the following table to find the suitable biological gender segment.

since both the "male param" and "female param" can squish into 1 syllable and get /no/, /na/, /to/, /ta/,
we can write them as a single digit in [[🟡 04 - Prime Factor Script]]


|      | /n-/                       | /t-/        |
| ---- | -------------------------- | ----------- |
| /-o/ | /no/ : not male nor female | /to/ : male |
| /-a/ | /na/ : female              | /ta/ : both |

>[!note] Pronunciation derivation
>According to biology (so far), male gametes moves, female gametes stay,
> derived from this,
>
in a syllable, consonants require your oral parts to move, and vowels require your oral parts to stay in place. Therefore we can have the consonant parts giving information about the male parameter and vowels giving the female parameter.
>
the inventory we have here, in this case, is "n,l,d,t" and "o, i, æ, a", 
"completely not male or female" is genderless,
"full blast male" will be "t"
"full blast female" will be "a"

>[!warning] 
>"no" which means genderless, most of the time for non-living animate objects,
> "ta" which means both, does not mean "bisexual" or "binary" or "pan", it means both at the same time, normal humans do not have both biological genders, however, plants that have flowers do (anthers are male parts and the ovary is a female part).
