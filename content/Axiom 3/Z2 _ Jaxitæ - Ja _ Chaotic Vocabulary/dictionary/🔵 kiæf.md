noun
1. knife
2. a tool used to cut

verb
1. /krut-kiæf-pi/ : cut, separate
2. /krut-kiæf-xa/ : slice
3. /krut-kiæf-tok/ : dice, mince
4. /krut-kiæf-pæk/ : smash *(Uncle Roger's favourite move on Garlic or Shallot)*

in mathematics, /kiæf/ is used for fractions,
$\frac{A}{B}$ is pronunciated as /kiæf A ru B/
