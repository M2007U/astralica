Normally in a sentence, a noun is unavoidable.
In normal sense, the Subject, Medium and Object in a sentence are Nouns, or NounClauses.

Under some circumstances, different root words can become nouns.

The symbol is derived from the shape of Tags.
These tags usually appear on Objects or People, perfect for both /Kosrano/es and /Kosrani/es

>[!note] Evolution Tracking
>/Tæga/ can be treated as a derivation of [[🟡 01 _ KosraNo - Ko _ Object]] and [[🟡 01 _ KosraNi - Koi - Living Beings]]

This symbol can represent "Name" / "Noun".

>[!note] pronunciation derivation
>Inspired by the word "Tag" from English,
>after "Japanize beaming" it
>we have /Tæga/

## Turning Events into Things (a.k.a. Gerund / nounform / NounClause)

Sometimes we can turn the whole sentence into a noun,
which has the meaning of "Take this entire event, and treat it as a noun".

We do this by encapsulating the entire sentence with /Tæræf/ and /Tæraf/

These 2 particles are derived from "Tæga" and "KaxiRæ" and "KaxiRa" (refer to [[🟡 01 _ Kaxi - Ka _ Space]])
