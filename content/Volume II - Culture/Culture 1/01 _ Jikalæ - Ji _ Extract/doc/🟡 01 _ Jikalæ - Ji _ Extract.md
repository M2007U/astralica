**loan words**
for now Astralica is not perfectly complete,
from time to time we can loan words from other Natural languages like English, Japanese etc.
these words are usually context heavy and arbituary
any words that are in this category will have the /Ji/ prefix

**"to extract, to pick, to collect"**
the word /Jikalæ/ also has the meaning of "to extract" or "to pick up"
it can be simply picking up an object from the ground
plucking an apple from a tree
citing a source from the internet
importing a library into your code

>[!note] Glyph Derivation
>derived from the shape of a hand picking something

>[!note] Pronunciation Derivation
>from Chinese "Jie"
>from Japanese "Kairu"
>from English "Lend"
